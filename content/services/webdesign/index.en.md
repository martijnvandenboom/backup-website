I build websites that not only look good but also run smoothly. The preference is for static HTML websites. This service is offered on a voluntary basis, with the option of a voluntary contribution.

## What I offer

In web design and development I believe in the power of simplicity.

**Beautiful static HTML websites.** Sites that put your brand and content in the spotlight, simple and elegant.

**Made to measure.** Every project starts with a real understanding of your goals. The website matches what you need.

**Fast and reliable.** The sites are fast and reliable, for a solid visitor experience.

## How it works

1. **Describe your vision.** Tell me about the project and the goals. How do you see the website? Which features do you need?
2. **Design and development.** We work together on design and build until the vision is real.
3. **Review and adjustments.** After the first version we discuss it and change what needs changing.
4. **Publication.** The website is prepared for launch.

Want the project to land as a clean static HTML website? Send a message and we can see what fits.
