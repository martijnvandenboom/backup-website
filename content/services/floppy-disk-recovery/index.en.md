Recovery of 5.25", 3.5" floppies and Iomega Zip Disks (super floppy) — a voluntary service.

Do you have old floppy disks lying around with precious memories and important documents? Those disks are often worth more than they look. That is why I offer floppy disk recovery.

## What I offer

**Recovery of 5.25", 3.5" and Zip floppy disks.** Experience and the right equipment to recover data from disks that may have seemed lost. They are handled with care.

These disks often hold personal and historical information. The work is aimed at keeping those memories.

## How it works

Send a message describing your situation and the floppy disks you want recovered. You will get a reply as soon as possible, and we work out the best approach together.

Feel free to ask questions. The aim is to preserve what is on those disks.
