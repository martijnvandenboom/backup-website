## The problem

The folders are full. Phone exports, old MPGs, scanned prints.

Nobody puts them in order. Nobody makes something you can watch in one sitting.

So they sit there — until someone needs a gift for parents, an anniversary, or a farewell.

## What you get

You get one compilation film of about 15 minutes, 1920×1080, 16:9, with quiet transitions. Suitable for TV, laptop, or projector.

### In the film

- Optional: your opening clip at the start.
- After that, everything in real capture order (from the photo and video metadata, not the copy date).
- Every photo is included, on screen for about 4 seconds.
- A short excerpt from every video; the total running time stays around 15 minutes.
- Original sound stays on the videos.
- Under the stills: tracks you choose (for example one song in the first half, another after the midpoint).

### What you receive

A playable file. No app, no account, no folder of hundreds of loose files.

## How it works

1. **Send the files.** Folder, drive, or export: JPG, PNG, MOV, MP4, old camcorder files, scans. A short note: the occasion, an opening clip if you want one, and your music preference.
2. **Sort, time, underscore.** I put everything on capture date, choose excerpts so the film stays within a quarter of an hour, and lay your tracks under the stills.
3. **Preview.** You watch a version. Small notes can still go in: opening clip, a music change, something that really must not be missing.
4. **Final film.** You receive the finished file, ready to play.

## Who it’s for / when

For adult children who want to give their parents something you can watch together.

For families with boxes of tapes and twenty years of phone dumps.

For a birthday, anniversary, retirement, a child leaving home, a reunion.

Not for anyone looking for a filmmaker on location. Yes for anyone who says: we have hundreds of files, and nobody watches them.

## Why this is not a slideshow app

An app dumps everything in a folder and lets it flash by. Here the order of when it happened counts, the time per photo, the piece from each video so the whole thing fits in one fifteen-minute sitting, the sound that belongs on the film, and the tracks you want under the stills. The result is one film — not a queue of thumbnails.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which files can I send?

Photos (JPG, PNG, scans) and video (including iPhone MOV/MP4 and older camcorder files such as MPG/WMV). If you are unsure about a format, send a sample.

### How long does it take?

That depends on how many files there are and whether the dates in the files are correct. After I receive them, you get a realistic timeline. Do not count on “ready tomorrow”; do count on careful work.

### What about music rights?

You supply the tracks (or a clear preference). Rights for home viewing or a gift are yours to arrange. I place the tracks under the stills; I do not supply a commercial music licence.

### What happens to our privacy?

I use the files only for this film. No publication, no samples without permission. After completion we agree how long the source material stays and when it is deleted.

### How do you decide how long a video clip lasts?

Every video is included. The length of the excerpt depends on how many films there are, so the whole piece stays around 15 minutes. No arbitrary cuts to make it “exciting” — just a workable whole.

### Can we choose the opening clip ourselves?

Yes. Say which file should go first. The rest follows in real capture order.
