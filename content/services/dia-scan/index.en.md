Digitising valuable slide collections so memories are kept and can be shared.

## What I offer

**High-quality digitisation.** Colour accuracy and detail when converting slides into digital files.

**Safety and care.** Slides are handled carefully and kept safe throughout the process.

**Made to fit.** You choose digital formats and resolutions that match how you want to use them.

## How it works

1. **Contact.** Send a message with your interest and questions. The next steps follow from there.
2. **Scanning.** The slides are digitised with suitable equipment.
3. **Quality check.** Each digital scan is checked for quality and accuracy.

The aim is to make slide collections usable again. Send a message when you want to start.
